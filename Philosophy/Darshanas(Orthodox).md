The Six Orthodox Schools (Darshanas)

- **[[Samkhya]]:** An early dualist system dividing reality into consciousness (_purusha_) and matter (_prakriti_).

- **[[Yoga]]:** A practical path using meditation and physical discipline, based on Samkhya ideas.

- **[[Nyaya]]:** A strict school focused on logic, reason, and valid ways to gain true knowledge.

- **[[Vaisheshika]]:** A natural science approach teaching atomism and the physical makeup of the world.

- **[[Mimamsa]]:** A system focused on proper ritual duties, ethics, and the correct interpretation of Vedic texts.

- **[[Vedanta]]:** The culmination of Vedic thought exploring the oneness or relationship between the individual soul and Brahman.
